NURA SKIN WEBSITE
=================

Open index.html in your browser.

Files:
- index.html — landing page
- style.css — complete styling
- script.js — products, menu and FAQ interactions
- products/ — 4 individual product detail pages
- images/ — your supplied product and logo images

Notes:
- Replace the placeholder social links and WhatsApp number with your real accounts.
- Prices are sample placeholders and can be edited in script.js and product detail pages.
- The Buy Now buttons currently open an email draft. Connect them to your preferred checkout/WhatsApp order flow when ready.
